# Monolithic Architecture - Trade-offs
## Pros, Cons, and System Constraints

### Pros
- Easy to develop, test, and deploy.
- Strong transactional guarantees (ACID).

### Cons
- Scales as a whole, not by component.
> [!IMPORTANT]
> - Large codebase MUST slow down IDEs and startup times.
